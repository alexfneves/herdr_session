4. If a lane reports "completed" — **verify the artifact exists before believing it.**

  `too_small_message()` at `:68`.
- **The `%`-only format string is deliberate** (Odin can't mix `{}` and `%`; `{:.1}` renders

## APP WAS NOT GATE-GREEN — 444 was launcher-dependent (RESOLVED: now 446 gate-green from `env -i`)

The `app` row is **444 pass / 0 fail when I run the binary from my herdr shell**, but **443 / 1 fail under the gate**.
Verified by direct A/B on the same binary:

| launch env | `we are running inside herdr (the scrub is not vacuous)` |
|---|---|
| inherited (`HERDR_ENV=1`) | **PASS** — `HERDR_ENV=1` |
| gate-equivalent (`-u HERDR_ENV`) | **FAIL** — `HERDR_ENV=` |

**The guard is correct; the harness is the defect.** `tests/tools/hscheck/envhelper.odin` deliberately strips the
entire `HERDR_*` namespace from every spawned suite (`support.base_env`), so that the raw developer environment
never reaches a suite. But `app_spawn_test` proves its scrub isn't vacuous by checking its **own inherited**
`HERDR_ENV` — which the gate has just removed. With the parent holding no `HERDR_*`, the child's
`leaked=[]` assertion is indeed vacuous, and the guard correctly refuses to let it count.

Consequences I must not paper over:
1. **The recorded `app` 444 is not gate-authoritative.** It holds only when launched from inside herdr. Do not
   fold `pass_after=444` as if the gate agreed.
2. `app_spawn_test` currently reports `pass=3 fail=1` under the gate, so the app row is **red under the very
   tool meant to certify it**.
3. This is exactly the class of bug the guard exists to catch — it fired on our own harness.

**Fix direction (do NOT weaken the guard):** make the vacuity proof independent of ambient env. The suite should
*arm* a known `HERDR_*` value itself before spawning the child, so the scrub has something deliberate to remove
and the guard can assert on what it armed rather than on luck. Then 444 is true under the gate, from any launcher.
Alternative would be the gate deliberately arming `HERDR_*` for the app row only — worse, because it puts a
suite's correctness into harness configuration.
